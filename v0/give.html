Giving questions

How do I calculate my tithe?

A tithe is the biblical principle of giving the first 10% of your income back to God (Malachi 3:10). If you're just starting, begin there and grow at your own pace.

What fund should I choose?

Tithes & Offerings supports the day-to-day ministry of the church. Building, Outreach, and Benevolence funds direct your gift to a specific purpose.

How do I edit my recurring gift?

Log into your giving account any time to change the amount, date, or payment method — or to pause a recurring gift.

Will I get a giving statement?

Yes — we email an updated statement each quarter. Make sure your contact info is current in your account.

Can I give if I'm outside the US?

Yes. International gifts can be made by major credit card through the online giving link — just leave the mobile field blank.
