Common questions

Who sees my prayer request?

Only our trusted prayer and care team. Requests are held in confidence and never shared publicly without your permission.

Can I stay anonymous?

Absolutely. Check the box on the form and we'll pray without any identifying details — you can still leave contact info if you'd like a follow-up.

Is there urgent or crisis support?

For a hospital visit, loss, or urgent need, note it in your request or call (512) 555-0142 and our care pastor will respond as soon as possible.

Can someone pray with me in person?

Yes. A prayer team is available at the front after both Sunday services — no appointment needed, just come find us.
