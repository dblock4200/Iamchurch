Meet our pastors

Alex & Sem Araiza

Together, Alex and Sem have led I AM since its first gathering around a living-room table in 2003. They're parents to three, neighbors on Grace Avenue, and stubborn believers that the church is at its best when its doors are widest.

Alex teaches most Sundays; Sem leads our outreach and care ministries. Between them they've spent two decades learning the same lesson they preach — that grace keeps showing up.
