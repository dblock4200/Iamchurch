Why groups

Faith was never meant to be solo.

A group is a dozen people who read the Scriptures together, pray for each other by name, and show up when life gets hard. It's where "I AM" stops being a place you attend and becomes people you belong to.

  • A dozen people who actually know your name and your story.
  • Scripture and honest conversation, not a lecture.
  • Someone in your corner when life gets heavy — and to celebrate when it doesn't.
Could this be you?

You don't need to be an expert — just willing to open your door.

We'll train you, give you the material, and walk with you. Hosting a group is the most ordinary, world-changing thing you can do with a Tuesday night.
