# Updating the I AM Church website

Everything on this page takes a phone or a laptop and about two minutes.
You cannot break the website from here. If something looks wrong, close the
tab and nothing is lost.

## Signing in

Go to **https://iamchurchaz.sanity.studio** and sign in with your email.

Bookmark it. That address is the only one you need.

Down the left side you'll see:

- **Events**
- **Sunday messages**
- **Groups**
- **Photo albums**
- **Service times & announcement**

## Posting an event

1. Click **Events**, then the **+** at the top.
2. Fill in the event name, the date and time, and where it is.
3. Add a photo if you have one. Sideways phone pictures are fine — they get
   straightened and cropped automatically.
4. Under **Show under these filters**, tick what fits. Most things are just
   "Event". Tick "Volunteer" if you need helpers, "Featured" to highlight it.
5. Click **Publish** (bottom right).

It's on the website straight away. Refresh the events page to see it.

**You do not need to delete old events.** Once an event's date has passed it
comes off the website by itself.

## Posting Sunday's message

**Sunday messages** → **+** → title, who preached, the date, and paste the
YouTube or Facebook link. Publish.

Leave the video link blank if there isn't one — it still posts.

## Adding photos

**Photo albums** → **+** → give the album a name like "Fall Picnic 2026",
then drag your photos into the Photos box. You can drop in a whole batch at
once and walk away while they upload.

## Changing service times

**Service times & announcement** → change the times → Publish.

## Putting a notice at the top of the site

Same page. Turn on **Show the announcement bar**, type one short sentence,
and publish. It appears across the top of every page.

Turn the switch off when it's no longer needed. Please do turn it off — a
notice that's been up for a month stops being read.

## The two rules

1. **Nothing is live until you press Publish.** A half-finished event sits
   there as a draft and nobody sees it.
2. **Anything you publish, you can change.** Open it, edit it, publish again.
   There's no version of this where you're stuck.

## If something goes wrong

The website keeps showing whatever it last knew, so a bad moment here never
takes the site down. Call Damien.
