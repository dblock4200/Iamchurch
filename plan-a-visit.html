Good questions

Before you come

What should I wear?

Whatever's comfortable. You'll see everything from jeans to Sunday best — nobody's counting.

Where do I park?

Free on-site parking off Grace Avenue. A team in orange vests will wave you into the lot.

What about my kids?

Secure, background-checked check-in for newborns through 5th grade every Sunday.

How early should I arrive?

Doors open thirty minutes early. Come a bit ahead for coffee and to get kids checked in.
