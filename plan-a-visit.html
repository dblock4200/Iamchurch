Your first Sunday

What to expect

No matter where you are in your faith walk, you’re welcome here. Come as you are.

Arrive & get settled

Doors open at 1:30 PM, thirty minutes before service. Come a little early, find a seat, and say hi. We’ll be glad to meet you.

Worship together

We open with worship music. Sing along or simply take it in — there’s no pressure either way.

A message from God’s Word

Pastor Sem teaches most Sundays: clear, practical teaching from the Bible you can carry into your week.

Kids are welcome

Kids join their parents in worship, then head to their Sunday class.

Good questions

Before you come

What should I wear?

Whatever's comfortable. You'll see everything from jeans to Sunday best.

What about my kids?

Kids join their parents in worship, then go to their Sunday class after worship.

How early should I arrive?

Doors open thirty minutes early, and service starts at 2:00 PM.

Do I have to sign up first?

No — everyone is welcome, sign-up or not. Letting us know just helps us be ready to welcome you.
